# Third-party notices

Rainloft's sounds are loops edited from real field recordings. Each remains under its own licence.
Edits: a section of each recording was selected, crossfaded into a seamless loop and
loudness-normalised; the thunder recordings had the rain underneath removed; drones and coloured
noise are synthesized. The same notices ship inside the app (Help ▸ Sound Credits).
